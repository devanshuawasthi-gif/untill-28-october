Privacy notice

This notice explains how information is handled when you request the free PDF of Until 28 October.

What is collected?

If you submit the download form, the website records the name and email address you provide, a generated record ID, the date and time of the request, the book title, and the status “Download Requested.” The site does not verify your email address and does not intentionally collect device fingerprints or unrelated personal information.

Why is it collected?

The details are recorded to maintain a basic record of voluntary book-download requests and to help the website owner understand requests. The CSV record does not prove that the file was opened or read.

Where is it stored?

Records are stored in a CSV file on the server running this Flask application, in a non-public data folder. The CSV is not exposed as a public download link. The website owner can retrieve it through a protected admin page.

Your choices and contact

Important before deployment

Hosting providers may process technical data such as IP addresses in their own server logs. This starter application does not deliberately log submitted email addresses. Review your hosting provider's privacy practices and adapt this notice to your actual setup and applicable law.
