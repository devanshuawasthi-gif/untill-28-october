Dear you,

Sabse pehle, happy birthday! ❤️ Aaj ka din tumhare liye utna hi khoobsurat ho jitni khoobsurati tum apne aas-paas ki duniya mein laati ho. I hope tumhari subah ek smile ke saath shuru ho, tumhare din mein chhoti-chhoti khushiyan aayein, aur raat ko jab tum apne din ke baare mein socho, toh dil halka aur khush mehsoos ho.

Kabhi-kabhi zindagi mein kuch log bina kisi grand entry ke aate hain, lekin dheere-dheere woh hamari soch, hamari yaadon aur hamari kahani ka ek special hissa ban jaate hain. Unke saath hui chhoti si baat bhi yaad reh jaati hai. Ek ordinary moment bhi baad mein sochne par special lagta hai. Shayad kisi insaan ki ahmiyat isi tarah samajh aati hai—not through big promises, but through the quiet way they stay in your thoughts.

Tum mere liye aisi hi ek yaad ka hissa bani. Main yeh nahi kehta ki tumhe meri feelings ko samajhna hi hoga, ya tumhe unka koi jawab dena chahiye. Feelings kabhi-kabhi bas hoti hain; unhe kisi par zimmedari banakar rakhna sahi nahi hota. Main sirf itna kehna chahta hoon ki tumhari presence ne meri kahani ko kuch aise rang diye jinhe main shabdon mein samajhne aur sambhalne ki koshish karta raha.

Ek tarfa mohabbat ka apna ek alag sa ehsaas hota hai. Ismein kuch baatein kahi jaati hain, kuch dil mein hi reh jaati hain, aur kuch yaadein waqt ke saath aur gehri hoti chali jaati hain. Kabhi ek chhoti si conversation bhi kaafi lagti hai; kabhi bahut kuch kehne ke baad bhi lagta hai ki sabse zaroori baat abhi baaki hai. Lekin maine yeh bhi seekha hai ki kisi ko dil se chahna aur uski azaadi ki izzat karna ek saath mumkin hai. Tumhari khushi, tumhari choices aur tumhara sukoon apni jagah poore aur important hain.

Isi liye maine Until 28 October likhne ki koshish ki. Yeh sirf ek title ya ek date nahi hai. Mere liye yeh un feelings ko words dene ka ek tareeka hai jo shayad saamne baith kar kabhi theek se keh nahi paata. Ismein first sight jaise ehsaas, unsaid feelings, yaadein, late-night conversations aur ek adhoori si kahani ka rang hai. Yeh meri taraf se ek personal expression hai—not a request, not a promise you need to answer, and never a burden you have to carry.

Ho sakta hai har kahani ka ending waise na ho jaisa hum kabhi imagine karte hain. Kuch kahaniyan complete answer ke bina bhi meaningful rehti hain. Kuch log hamari life mein ek chapter ki tarah aate hain, aur woh chapter humein apne baare mein kuch sikha jaata hai: patience, honesty, softness, aur kisi ko bina shart khush dekhne ki wish. Main apni feelings ko ek respectful aur beautiful memory ki tarah sambhalna chahta hoon.

Aaj tumhare birthday par meri wish bahut simple hai: may life be kind to you. Tumhe aise log milen jo tumhari baat dhyaan se sunein, tumhari boundaries samjhein, tumhari achievements celebrate karein, aur difficult days mein tumhe yaad dilayein ki tum akeli nahi ho. Tumhare dreams ko jagah mile, tumhari mehnat rang laaye, aur tumhe woh peace mile jo sirf achhe din nahi, balki tough days mein bhi saath rahe.

I hope tum apne aap par proud feel karo—sirf tab nahi jab tum kuch achieve karo, balki un dinon mein bhi jab tum bas koshish kar rahi hoti ho. I hope tumhe hamesha apni value yaad rahe, aur tum kabhi apni smile ko kisi aur ki expectations ke liye change na karo. Tumhe apni life apne tareeke se jeene ka poora haq hai, aur main dil se chahta hoon ki tumhara raasta tumhe khushi aur growth ki taraf le jaaye.

Agar kabhi tum is book ko padho, toh ise ek honest piece of writing ki tarah padhna. Ismein jo bhi emotions hain, woh mere hain; unka matlab yeh nahi ki tum par koi expectation hai. Tum ise apni marzi se padho, skip karo, ya bas ek gift ki tarah rakh do. Your comfort matters. Main chahta hoon ki yeh gesture tumhare birthday ko thoda sa warm banaye, na ki tumhe kisi jawab ke baare mein sochne par majboor kare.

Thank you for being a part of the story that inspired these pages, even in ways you may not realise. Kuch yaadein waqt ke saath badal jaati hain, lekin unse judi learning aur gratitude reh sakti hai. Main unhi feelings ko ek aisi jagah rakhna chahta hoon jahan unhe softness ke saath dekha ja sake—without pressure, without regret, and with respect.

So, on your special day, may your heart feel light, your laughter come easily, and your future hold more beautiful surprises than you can imagine. May you find courage when life feels uncertain, calm when things get loud, and people who appreciate you for exactly who you are. May this new year of your life bring you closer to the things that make you feel truly alive.

Happy Birthday once again. Keep smiling, keep growing, and keep choosing the life that feels right for you.
Some stories may remain unfinished, but the good wishes can always be complete.

With warmth and good wishes,
Devanshu
